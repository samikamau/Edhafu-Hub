Why Edhafu

Built by people who keep books for a living.

Edhafu comes out of years of reconstructing records for SMEs, SACCOs and Chamas. Every screen is designed to prevent the gaps we used to fix by hand.

Made for Kenya

Audit-ready records

Private by design

Computer or phone
